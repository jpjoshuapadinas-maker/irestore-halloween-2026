Hair education

Why does hair thin?

Hair thinning can have many causes. Understanding the reason behind changes in your hair is an important first step.

🧬

Genetics

Hereditary pattern hair loss is common and can affect people of different genders.

🔄

Life changes

Hormonal shifts, illness, medications, and aging can influence hair growth and shedding.

🌱

Health and nutrition

Stress, nutritional deficiencies, and some medical conditions may contribute to shedding.

Sudden, patchy, or unexplained hair loss deserves evaluation by a qualified healthcare professional.

The technology

What is low-level light therapy?

Low-level light therapy (LLLT) uses specific wavelengths of light. Some light-based devices have evidence supporting improvements in hair density for certain types of pattern hair loss, although results vary.

iRestore offers wearable light-therapy devices designed for use at home. Device-specific directions, indications, and safety information should always guide use.

Set realistic expectations

Consistency over magic.

Hair changes typically take time to assess. Not every cause of hair loss responds to light therapy, and results depend on individual circumstances.

What to look for

Track your routine, take photos in consistent lighting if helpful, and discuss ongoing concerns with a professional.

What not to expect

No device can guarantee regrowth, and a seasonal discount should never replace an informed decision.

Common questions

Before you begin

Is light therapy suitable for every kind of hair loss?

No. The evidence and device indications are specific; a healthcare professional can help assess the cause of hair loss.

How quickly will I see results?

Responses vary and generally require consistent use over time. Consult the official product guidance for realistic expectations.

Can I use iRestore alongside other treatments?

Ask your healthcare professional about combining treatments, especially if you use medication or have a scalp condition.
